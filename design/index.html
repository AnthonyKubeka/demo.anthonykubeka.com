Design language · version 1

How Normandy looks

Every Normandy page uses one stylesheet, 3 typefaces and 3 knobs. This page is the reference. It loads that stylesheet and nothing else, so what you see here is what a page gets.

01

Rules

Rule 1

One hot colour

Red marks what is selected, what is wrong and the one thing I want you to look at. Everything else is cool or grey.

Rule 2

Hairlines and cut corners

A panel is a 1 px line with 2 corners cut at 45°. I use no shadows, no glow and no rounded boxes.

Rule 3

Wide type for names only

The wide typeface is for names and short titles. A sentence is always set in the plain one.

Rule 4

Data is monospace

Every number, time and label uses the monospace, and numbers line up in a right-hand column.

Rule 5

State has a word

A lamp always sits next to the word for its state. Colour is never the only signal.

Rule 6

Three knobs

The hot hue, the frame hue and the corner cut are variables. Every other colour follows from them.

Use it

Copy the design/ folder next to your page and link design/normandy.css. It carries the fonts, the tokens and the parts on this page. Each part is one class that starts with nd-.

Michroma is by Vernon Adams, under the SIL Open Font License. NK57 Monospace is by Ray Larabie, who released it into the public domain. Both licence files ship in design/fonts/.
